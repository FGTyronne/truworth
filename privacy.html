Privacy Notice

Your data should make the decision clearer, not create surprises.

This notice explains what TruWorth collects, why it is used, how personalised recommendations work, who may process data and the choices available to you.

Last updated 14 October 2026

1. Controller and contact

TruWorth is operated by Ramella Corporate Consulting Ltd, Company No. 14295568, registered in England and Wales. For privacy enquiries or rights requests, contact partnerships@ramellacorporateconsulting.com.

2. Information we may collect

Account informationEmail address, authentication identifiers, account preferences and subscription status.
Assessment informationProducts considered, retailer URLs, price, currency, expected use, enjoyment, motivations, alternatives, scores and purchase/check-in history.
Financial profileWhere you choose to provide it: income, essential outgoings, debt commitments, savings targets and related affordability context.
Behavioural profileDecision Discipline, impulse tendency, buyer's-remorse tendency, upgrade behaviour, status sensitivity, quality preference and goal focus.
Subscription and billing recordsPlan, trial and billing status. Payment-card processing is handled by Stripe; TruWorth is not intended to receive or store your full payment-card number.
Technical and security dataDevice/browser information and service, authentication or security logs generated by our infrastructure where needed to operate and protect TruWorth.
Consent and preference recordsCookie/privacy choices, reduced-motion preferences and consent to use profile data for personalised recommendation features.
CommunicationsMessages you send us about support, privacy, billing, security or partnerships.

3. Where information comes from

Most information comes directly from you. Product information may also be obtained from public retailer/product pages when you ask TruWorth to import a URL. Subscription and payment status may be received from our payment provider. Technical/security information may be generated by the services used to deliver TruWorth.

4. Purposes and lawful bases

We use personal data only where we have a lawful basis. The basis depends on the activity.

Provide the serviceAccount access, assessments, saving history, comparisons and subscriptions: generally necessary to perform the service you request.
Personalised recommendationsWhere enabled, financial and behavioural profile data is used to personalise decision support. TruWorth requires an explicit in-product choice before activating this feature.
Security and abuse preventionUsed where necessary for our legitimate interests in protecting users, accounts, infrastructure and the integrity of the service, and where required by law.
Billing and recordsUsed to perform the subscription contract and meet legal, accounting, tax or dispute-resolution obligations.
Optional analytics/marketingWhere required, based on consent through the cookie/privacy preference centre.
Service improvementWe may use limited service and product-interaction information where necessary for legitimate interests in maintaining and improving TruWorth, balanced against user privacy.

5. Profiling and automated decision support

TruWorth can use assessment, financial and behavioural information to calculate scores and personalised recommendations such as BUY, WAIT, SKIP or NEITHER. This is profiling in the broad data-protection sense because personal information is used to evaluate preferences, behaviour or economic context.

TruWorth does not make a lending, credit, employment, insurance or legal decision about you.The recommendation is purchase decision support for you to consider. It does not itself produce a legal effect or bind you or a third party.

The public formula describes the categories considered, while protected coefficients and thresholds are kept server-side. You can change the profile inputs, change your Decision Discipline setting, decline personalised recommendation use, or ask us about the data used to create your profile. If you believe a result is wrong, you can challenge it or request clarification by contacting us.

6. Data minimisation

TruWorth is designed to use decision-relevant information rather than collecting broad financial records. Unless a future feature clearly says otherwise, we do not need your online-banking password, full bank statements, full card number or investment portfolio to provide the current purchase-decision service.

7. Service providers and recipients

Data may be processed by service providers supporting TruWorth, currently including Supabase for authentication/database/backend functions, Vercel for web deployment/hosting, Stripe for subscription payments, and Resend where transactional email is enabled. Other infrastructure may be used where necessary to operate or migrate the service. Retailers and external sites are independent third parties when you follow their links.

We do not permit a partner's commercial payment, affiliate commission or sponsorship to change your TruWorth recommendation score.

8. International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for a restricted transfer, we expect the relevant provider or contractual arrangement to use a recognised mechanism such as adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement/addendum, or another lawful safeguard as applicable.

9. Retention

Account and saved assessment data is generally retained while your account remains active and until you delete it, subject to backups, dispute, fraud/security, tax/accounting or other legal retention needs. Billing and transaction records may be retained for periods required by law or our payment/accounting obligations. Security logs are retained according to operational need and provider retention settings. Guest data and consent choices may remain in browser storage until you delete browser data, use available deletion controls or the stored data is replaced.

10. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and rights relating to consent and automated processing. Rights depend on the lawful basis and the nature of the processing.

Right to object: where we rely on legitimate interests, you may object to processing in circumstances provided by law. You can also object to direct marketing at any time.

Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw that consent for future processing without affecting prior lawful processing.

11. Cookies and browser storage

See the Cookie Policy for current browser-storage categories and controls. Non-essential analytics or marketing technologies should not be enabled before the required choice is made.

12. Security

We use technical and organisational controls appropriate to the nature of the service and continue to improve them as TruWorth develops. See Security for our current approach and responsible-disclosure route.

13. Complaints

Please contact us first if you have a privacy concern. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office where applicable.

14. Changes to this notice

We review this notice as the service, providers and law change. Material new uses of personal data should be brought to users' attention before the new processing begins where required.
