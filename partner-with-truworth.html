Partner with TruWorth

Reach people while they are deciding what is actually worth buying.

TruWorth is building a consumer decision platform around purchase value, financial discipline and personalised decision support. Commercial relationships are kept structurally separate from recommendation scoring.

Advertising on TruWorth

Free-tier placements are deliberately limited and visually restrained. Advertisers can appear in clearly identified partner spaces without interrupting the assessment or decision explanation.

Light display placementOne restrained partner banner in eligible Free-tier areas.
Category relevanceTechnology, home, lifestyle, fitness, travel and other relevant consumer categories.
Launch partner ratesEarly commercial packages are agreed directly rather than published as a fixed rate card.
Clear disclosurePaid placements are labelled and do not masquerade as TruWorth recommendations.

Member Benefits partners

Partners can offer TruWorth+ members a genuine benefit such as a discount, enhanced service, repair offer, trade-in benefit, warranty extension or another practical perk. Benefits must be real, current and capable of verification before publication.

Recommendation independence

Commercial relationships do not influence TruWorth scores or recommendations.Affiliate commission, sponsorship, placement fees or partner status are not inputs to the decision engine.

Broader partnerships

We are also open to selected educational, commercial, research and distribution partnerships where there is a credible fit with smarter consumer decision-making.

Partnership standards

Useful to usersThe proposition needs a credible consumer benefit, not merely paid visibility.
Clearly disclosedSponsored and affiliate relationships are identified where they appear.
Independent scoringNo commercial arrangement can buy a better TruWorth result.
