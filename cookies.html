Cookies & browser storage

You choose the optional stuff.

This policy explains the browser technologies TruWorth uses now, what may be introduced later, and how you can change your choice.

Last updated 15 October 2026

1. What this policy covers

Cookies are small files stored on a device. Websites can also use similar technologies such as local storage, session storage and software-development-kit storage. UK rules apply to storing information on, or accessing information from, a user's device even where the technology is not literally a cookie.

2. Our consent-first approach

TruWorth may use storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service you request, protect the service, maintain authentication, remember a privacy choice or preserve essential settings. Optional analytics, advertising and partner-measurement technologies are not intended to activate until you have made a valid choice permitting the relevant category.

Rejecting optional technologies does not remove core TruWorth access.Essential authentication, security and requested app functions may still use necessary browser storage.

3. Technologies currently used

Temporary TruWorth guest storageGuest assessments, temporary result data, owned-product tracking and core guest preferences are kept in session storage so requested app functions can work before sign-in. This storage is intended to remain temporary to the browsing session rather than becoming a persistent guest purchase history. Legacy persistent guest-state entries are removed by the current storage layer.
TruWorth consent recordStores your cookie/privacy choice, consent version and the time the choice was saved so we do not repeatedly ask for the same choice.
Authentication/session storageSupabase authentication may use browser storage to keep a signed-in session active and refresh it securely.
Security and infrastructureHosting, authentication and payment providers may use strictly necessary technologies to deliver, secure or complete services you request. The server-side daily guest quota is described in the Privacy Notice; its derived quota identifier is not a browser cookie.

4. Optional analytics

TruWorth's preference centre includes an Analytics category so optional measurement can be introduced without changing the consent model. Optional analytics should remain off unless you allow that category. When a specific analytics provider is enabled, this policy should be updated with its purpose, provider, identifiers and relevant duration before or at activation.

5. Optional marketing and partner measurement

This category may in future support advertising, affiliate attribution or measurement of partner campaigns. It is separate from TruWorth's recommendation engine. Partner or affiliate economics must not change a TruWorth score or recommendation. Optional marketing/partner measurement should remain off unless you allow that category.

6. Your choices

You can accept optional categories, reject all non-essential categories, or choose categories individually. You can change your choice at any time.

7. Withdrawing consent

Changing your choice applies going forward. You can also delete stored data using your browser controls. Deleting the TruWorth consent record may cause the consent notice to appear again. Browser-level blocking can affect essential sign-in or requested app functions.

8. Legal framework

We manage cookies and similar technologies with reference to the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, UK data-protection law and current Information Commissioner's Office guidance. Where consent is required, it should be freely given, specific, informed and indicated by a clear positive action.

9. Changes

If TruWorth materially changes the optional purposes or technologies described here, we may update the consent version and ask you to choose again.
