Privacy

Your data should help you, not surprise you.

This notice explains what TruWorth collects, why we use it and the choices you have.

Last updated 9 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

TruWorth is operated by Ramella Corporate Consulting Ltd, Company No. 14295568, registered in England and Wales. For privacy enquiries, contact partnerships@ramellacorporateconsulting.com.

2. What we collect

Account dataEmail address, authentication identifiers and account preferences.
Purchase dataProducts or services you assess, prices, links, scores, expected use, motivations and purchase/check-in history.
Optional financial contextIncome, essential outgoings, debt commitments, savings targets and the currency in which you entered them.
Technical dataLimited security, reliability and service logs generated by our hosting and infrastructure providers.

3. Why we use information

We use account and assessment information to provide TruWorth, calculate scores, save your history, generate buyer-pattern insights and, for eligible subscribers, provide personalised purchase decision support. We use limited technical information to secure, maintain and troubleshoot the service.

Legal bases

Depending on the activity, we rely on performance of our contract with you to provide the service you request; our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving TruWorth; compliance with legal obligations; and consent where the law requires it for optional analytics, marketing or similar technologies.

4. Who processes data

We use service providers for hosting, authentication, database infrastructure and payments where enabled. These currently include Vercel, Supabase and Stripe for the relevant parts of the service. If optional analytics providers are enabled, this notice will be updated before those services are used for the relevant purpose.

Third-party product pages and retailer sites have their own privacy practices. Following those links takes you outside TruWorth.

5. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. Where required, we expect appropriate contractual or legal safeguards to be used for international transfers.

6. How long we keep data

Signed-in account data is generally kept while your account remains active or while needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or maintain security records. Guest assessments remain in your browser storage until you clear them. You can delete saved product data from Settings or permanently delete your TruWorth account from Settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to receive portable copies of certain information. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

To exercise a privacy right, contact partnerships@ramellacorporateconsulting.com.

8. Optional analytics and storage

TruWorth uses browser storage where necessary to remember guest assessments, preferences and signed-in sessions. Optional analytics that require consent should not be enabled until an appropriate choice mechanism is available. If that changes, the app and this notice will be updated.

9. Changes

We may update this notice as TruWorth changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
