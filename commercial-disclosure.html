Commercial disclosure

TruWorth decides first. Commercial relationships come second.

Advertising, sponsorship, affiliate commission and Partner Benefits are kept separate from TruWorth scoring and personalised recommendation logic.

Last updated 15 October 2026

1. The rule

A commercial relationship cannot buy a better TruWorth result.Partner status, sponsorship fees, affiliate commission, discounts and advertising spend are not inputs to the TruWorth scoring or recommendation model.

2. Advertising and sponsorship

Eligible Free-tier areas may contain restrained commercial placements. Paid placements should be identified with wording such as Sponsored, Partner or another clear disclosure appropriate to the relationship. TruWorth+ is intended to remain free from these advertising placements.

Commercial placements are not inserted inside the personalised recommendation itself. A partner cannot pay to change BUY, WAIT, SKIP or NEITHER.

3. Affiliate links

Some links may be affiliate links. If you follow an affiliate link and complete an eligible transaction, TruWorth or its operator may receive a commission or other commercial benefit from the third party. Where a link is used on that basis, the relationship should be disclosed alongside or close to the placement.

You do not pay TruWorth a higher purchase price merely because a link is an affiliate link, although the third party remains responsible for its own pricing, terms, availability and checkout.

4. Member Benefits

Partner Benefits may give eligible members a discount, trade-in benefit, warranty extension, service enhancement or another practical perk. They are shown after or separately from the decision process and are supplied by independent third parties under any applicable partner terms.

A benefit can make an already-chosen transaction more valuable, but its existence does not instruct the recommendation engine to tell you to buy.

5. Measurement and privacy

Optional partner-campaign measurement is controlled through the Marketing & partner measurement privacy preference. Where campaign measurement is enabled and you have allowed that category, TruWorth may record limited campaign events such as an impression or click. The current commercial event design does not require an advertising partner to receive your TruWorth financial profile, behavioural profile or personalised recommendation inputs.

You can change your choice at any time through . See the Cookie Policy and Privacy Notice.

6. Recommendation independence

The recommendation engine and commercial campaign system use separate data paths. Recommendation logic does not read the advertising campaign, affiliate event or partner-relationship tables. Commercial delivery does not read the financial or behavioural profile used by the personalised recommendation engine.

7. Questions or concerns

If you believe a commercial relationship has been presented unclearly or appears to compromise TruWorth's independence, contact partnerships@ramellacorporateconsulting.com.
